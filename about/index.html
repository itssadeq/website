The Story

I graduated first in my class. Best economics school in my country. Every award they had to give, I won it. And then something dangerous happened. I got comfortable.

Comfort is my compass. When I feel it, I move in the opposite direction.

I set my sights on a top ten university in the world. Two years later, I walked out of the London School of Economics with a degree in Financial Economics.

That opened the door to Klarna. At the time, the most valuable fintech company in Europe. Three years. Eighteen countries. Forty-nine million users. I managed enterprise accounts, developed business, optimized internal processes, and owned the data, the KPIs, and the performance analysis behind it all. I learned more in those three years than in all my degrees combined.

In parallel, another master's. Because I wanted to understand business models and innovation, not just economics.

And then I kept building ↓

Disorder → Structure

Operating Range

Each demanded a fundamentally different approach: different speed, different stakeholders, different definition of "done." That range isn't common.
